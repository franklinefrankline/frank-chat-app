Request My Data

Exercise your Right to Access under DPDP Section 11. You can immediately download a structured machine-readable JSON archive of your personal data or file a formal statutory access inquiry with the Data Protection Officer.

* Data archive includes: Account profile, active consents, sent message metadata, uploaded document index, and privacy request history. Passwords, cryptographic keys, and third-party contact communications are excluded for privacy protection.

Correct My Information

Exercise your Right to Correction under DPDP Section 12 to update inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data. For standard profile settings (display name, avatar, bio, theme), use the direct profile editor. For protected account attributes (such as verified registered email), submit a formal review request below.

* Correction requests are assigned to the compliance review team. Protected attributes are updated only after verification to prevent unauthorized account changes. Submitting a request does not immediately alter privileged records until approved.

Request Data Erasure

Exercise your Right to Erasure under DPDP Section 12. Submitting an erasure request initiates a formal fiduciary review against statutory retention requirements (including 180-day security audit logging obligations under CERT-In directives). Submitting a request does not immediately destroy your account or communications.
