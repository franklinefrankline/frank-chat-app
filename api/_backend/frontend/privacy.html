1 Statutory Framework & Scope

This Privacy Policy and Data-Use Notice governs the processing of digital personal data by FRANK Think (hereinafter "FRANK Think", "we", "us", or "the Platform"). This policy is formulated in direct compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (DPDP Rules) enacted by the Parliament of India.

Under the DPDP Act, you are recognized as a Data Principal — the individual to whom the personal data relates — and FRANK Think operates as the Data Fiduciary determining the purpose and means of processing your digital personal data.

Core Commitment: FRANK Think practices data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation, and uncompromising integrity. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data, nor do we perform behavioral tracking for third-party commercial advertisements.

5 Storage, Retention & Staged Account Erasure

Your personal data is securely stored within our production relational database (PostgreSQL) hosted in secure cloud infrastructure. Uploaded media and documents are stored in protected server storage with strict access authorization filters.

Retention Schedule: Personal data is maintained only for as long as your account remains active, or as required by statutory retention exceptions under Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act (such as compliance with legal obligations or dispute defense).

Staged Account Deletion Workflow: When you initiate self-service account deletion from your Privacy Dashboard:
  1. Your identity is verified via mandatory password confirmation.
  2. Active WebSocket sessions are terminated immediately.
  3. Personal identifiers (name, username, email, avatar, bio) are permanently anonymized.
  4. To protect conversation partners from arbitrary loss of context, message content in shared threads remains preserved under an anonymized author label ("Deleted User").
  5. Group channels created by the user are reassigned to system administrators to preserve group availability for other participating members.

7 Security & Encryption Architecture

FRANK Think implements rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Transport Encryption: 100% of network traffic, HTTP APIs, and WebSocket streams are encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3.
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are hashed using salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 rounds. Plaintext passwords are never recorded in logs or database tables.
  • Token Authentication: Sessions are secured through cryptographically signed HS256 JWT tokens with automatic expiration.
  • Authorization Controls & Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) Protections: Backend APIs enforce multi-tier participant verification on all chat threads and document downloads. Users cannot access unauthorized conversations or attachments.
  • Accurate Architectural Statement: In strict compliance with regulatory honesty, FRANK Think stores messages in its database to support multi-device synchronization and document collaboration; we do not make false claims of proprietary end-to-end encryption.

9 Age Verification & Protection of Children

Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023, Data Fiduciaries must observe strict duties before processing any personal data of children (defined as individuals under 18 years of age):

  • Service Intended for Adults: FRANK Think is designed for users aged 18 years and above. During registration, users are required to confirm their age.
  • Verifiable Parental Consent: If an individual under 18 registers, processing of their data requires verifiable parental consent pursuant to DPDP Rules.
  • No Detrimental Processing or Tracking: FRANK Think strictly prohibits behavioral tracking, targeted profiling, or any processing that could cause detrimental effects on the well-being of a child.
  • Privacy-Preserving Verification: In compliance with data minimization principles, FRANK Think does not collect government ID documents solely for general age verification without a demonstrated legal necessity.

10 Personal Data Breach Management & Notification

Under Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act, in the event of a personal data breach impacting confidentiality, integrity, or availability:

  1. Identification & Containment: Incidents are immediately registered in our internal breach management register and assigned to the incident response team.
  2. Impact Assessment: Forensic review determines the nature of the breach, affected systems, and number of potentially impacted Data Principals.
  3. Statutory Notification to DPBI: Notice is provided to the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with the timelines and format prescribed under the DPDP Rules.
  4. Notice to Affected Data Principals: Directly affected users are notified without unreasonable delay with clear remediation steps and recommended protective measures.
